**Sway** is an instrument you play with your hands.
One hand plays the melody, while the other shapes the band's energy and loops what you played.

The lead is synthesized in the browser, so gestures can become sound without waiting for a model.
A generative band responds to the notes and energy you provide, with a built-in band available when a model is late.

The public repository contains the playable V1, setup instructions, and the current development plans.
