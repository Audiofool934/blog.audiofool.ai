The [Moondog](/projects/moondog/) window now reads what is already playing in the Music app or Spotify. It can pause one service and continue on the other, while one player stays audible.

The lunar record is also a control: click the tonearm to play or pause, or turn the wheel to scratch and seek. Likes and Avoids can now come from chat, and the Avoid gate checks the songs Moondog sends. Its own suggestions stay separate from the listener's choices.
